# Standardized Customer Support Explanation: SBI Mutual Fund Exit Loads

**Purpose:** Reusable support snippet to clarify exit load charges on redemptions for Groww users.

---

### Understanding Exit Loads on Your Investment

* **What is an Exit Load?** An exit load is a nominal fee charged by the Asset Management Company (SBI Mutual Fund) when you redeem or switch out of fund units before a specified duration.
* **Standard Holding Threshold:** For most SBI equity schemes (e.g., SBI Flexicap Fund, SBI Large & Midcap Fund), an exit load of **1.00%** applies if units are redeemed within **1 year (365 days)** from the allotment date.
* **Nil Charges After 1 Year:** No exit load is charged on units held continuously for more than 365 days.
* **The First-In, First-Out (FIFO) Rule:** Redemptions follow a FIFO structure, meaning the units purchased earliest are always redeemed first.
* **SIP Calculation (Crucial):** For Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), **each monthly installment is treated as a fresh purchase** with its own independent 365-day timer.
* **Where Fees Go:** Exit load amounts are credited back directly to the mutual fund scheme portfolio to protect remaining long-term investors, as mandated by SEBI.
